# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

I aim to maintain a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone interacting with this portfolio.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Explain disagreements professionally.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, and improvements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption or abusive conduct.
- Sharing credentials or confidential information.

## Reporting Concerns

Please contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate channel to report conduct concerns.

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably as possible, with respect for privacy.
